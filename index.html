🖐 Palm Rejection

How it works
Palm rejection filters out accidental hand contacts by measuring the touch contact area. Large contacts (palm, heel of hand) are ignored, while small contacts (stylus tip, fingertip) are allowed to draw. Stylus and mouse inputs are always allowed.
Sensitivity

Higher = stricter filtering (blocks more contacts). Lower = permissive.
